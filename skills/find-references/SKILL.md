# Find references

Reference images for a new look: the person's own finds and open sources, chosen by the person and grouped into directions, one direction per Style draft.

**The rule: every draft gets references of its own, and the person picks them.** Drafts that reshuffle the same five pictures converge.

## Numbers

Measured on Blueprint Studio's MCP server.

- On 25 Styles run through the production generation path (12 to 13 Nov 2026), Styles made from reference images alone put the reference's subject into 42% of images. So pick references that share a look across different subjects, and search for the look, not the subject.
- Bad reference links were the most common external error, `INVALID_REFERENCE_IMAGE` and `REFERENCE_URL_UNREACHABLE` (28 Sep to 16 Oct 2026). Pass an image's own link, never the page it sits on.
- The finder and boards are new (Nov 2026): nothing measured on them yet.

## Steps

No `find_references` among your tools (search for it first if tools load on demand)? Follow Without the finder, below; with it but no `save_reference_board`, keep the board in the chat as that section says. In a brand, pass the same `brandId` on every call.

1. **Ask first.** In one message: what the look is for, which sources they already use (Pinterest, Mobbin, Are.na, Cosmos, sites they admire), and whether they have images or links of looks they love, or ones they don't. Take what they give as the table below says, noting where each came from. A page isn't an image. An Are.na channel goes to the finder (step 3). From Pinterest, Cosmos, Savee, Dribbble or Behance, never open the page. A Pinterest or Unsplash picture can't be a reference at all (step 2), but it can say what they like: ask what that is. From the other four, ask them to upload what they want, paste each image's own link, or bring it through their own connector for that site. A Mobbin page the same: through their Mobbin connector, or saved and uploaded. Any other page (not Unsplash) you may open to find the image's own link, or ask for the image. Nothing to give? Go on to step 3.
2. **Use the connectors they already have.** Where the host has their own connectors (a Pinterest connector, Mobbin's MCP, web search), search them for the look, worded as in step 3. Pinterest's and Unsplash's images are refused as references on every path, so their connectors and links only tell you the look: name what the pictures they point at share, and find it in the open sources or their own photos. Mobbin is screens of real apps: use it when the look is for an interface or what sits in one (onboarding illustrations, empty states, icons), search the kind of screen as Mobbin asks, and pick by look from what it shows; for print, photography or campaign looks, skip it and say why. They're signed in already: never ask for a password, key, token or cookie, and never put one in a call. A connector that isn't connected is theirs to connect in their app, or they upload instead. From web search, leave out anything on the five sites in step 1, Mobbin or Unsplash, their image hosts included (such as `i.pinimg.com` and `images.unsplash.com`). Show the finds you keep in step 4. Per source: [Sources](references/sources.md).
3. **Search the open sources.** `find_references` with a `query` that names a look, not a subject: medium, technique, light, palette, texture, era ("risograph print, two colors, heavy grain", not "coffee shop"). Leave `sources` out to ask Pexels, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons. Keep `count` at its default 12, so three rounds fit one board, and set `orientation` when the Style is for one shape. Read an Are.na channel they pasted in a call of its own: `arenaChannel` with its link or slug and no `query`; its next 12 (`page`) are a later round's. Say in one line which sources answered and which were skipped, with each skipped one's `note` (`sources.skipped`): Pexels while the service has no Pexels key, or a source resting after a bot check or its limit, until the time its note gives. Go on with those that answered, and don't call again for a skipped one sooner. When `sources.unfinished` names a source, make the same call again before saving any, as its `nextStep` says.
4. **Save the round, then show it.** Look at the contact sheet (some candidates may not be on it: Tiles missing from a sheet, below), and leave out near-duplicates, misses and anything already on the board (the same `sourceUrl`). `save_reference_board` with a fresh UUID `requestId` and the candidates you kept as `items`, in the table's form; on the first round also a `name`, a `brief` (the look and what it's for) and the person's own files and links; on later rounds only the `boardId` it returned (a `name` or `brief` sent with it changes nothing). Show the new finds in one message: the candidates under the board's numbers, and connector finds as C1, C2… with where each came from (each Mobbin screen as a link to its `mobbin_url`; not Pinterest's, which only tell you the look). Where Markdown images render, each `thumbUrl` as one; in a terminal, a numbered list of `thumbUrl` links. Each candidate's `attribution` goes under it, and the finder's `credits` once with them (Pexels asks for it wherever its photos are shown). Not the finder's own sheet: its numbers aren't the board's. Ask which feel right and which don't.
5. **Record what they said.** Connector finds they liked go on the board first: `save_reference_board` with a fresh `requestId`, the `boardId` (or, if this is the first save, a `name` and `brief`) and just those. Then `react_to_references` with the `boardId` and the board numbers they named as `likes` and `dislikes`, the connector likes included. Their own images are likes when they brought them as the look they want, dislikes when they brought them as what to avoid. Only their words; leave the rest undecided.
6. **Vary the next search from the likes.** Say in one line what the likes share ("all flat color with heavy grain"), then search again with a query that keeps that, changes one other thing, and steers clear of what the dislikes share. Not `page: 2` of the same query; that is more of the same. Steps 4 and 5 again, on the same board. Two or three rounds at most; stop sooner once the likes make two directions. With fewer than four likes, offer another round, their own images, or going on with what there is, and do what they choose; a fourth round only if they ask.
7. **Group the likes into 2 to 4 directions.** Each differs from the first in one thing (the medium, the palette, the light, the texture or the era), named in its label, holds 2 to 5 liked pictures, and shares none with another. Too few likes for two: one direction is one draft (a lone like can be its one reference, or ask for more), and the `styles` skill plans the others (with text, or from an existing Style). Show the directions with their labels and numbers, change them if the person says so, then `react_to_references` with `directions: [{ label, items }]`.
8. **Hand off to the `styles` skill, one direction per draft.** The board lists each direction's pictures as Style `inputs` (`summary.directions`, from `react_to_references` or `get_reference_board`), each keeping its `source`, `sourceUrl`, `licence`, `author`, `note` and `suppliedBy`. Each direction becomes one draft labelled like it, with those `inputs` as they are: five at most, none in two drafts. A direction whose uploads are `unavailable` is missing them: follow the board's `nextStep` for them first. The one shared set the `styles` skill may add is a direction's twin with text, to test the text. Then follow the `styles` skill: its prompts, the credit total said before anything runs, and the person's pick per prompt.
9. **Report.** The `boardId`, each direction and the draft it became, and the sources, licences and attribution behind them. Attribution stays with each reference, and `get_style` shows it; it isn't put in images.

## What each reference carries

Every reference keeps where it came from, from the board to the draft: `source` and `suppliedBy` as below on the board item and the Style input, a `sourceUrl` (the page it came from, else its own link), and `licence` and `author` whenever the source states them. A `sourceUrl` is only recorded, never fetched, so any http or https page can be one. A candidate's `attribution` is for showing the person: the board doesn't take it, and it never goes in a `note`; `get_style` makes each reference's credit from its source, author, licence and page.

| It is | Board item | Style input | `source` | `suppliedBy` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A `find_references` candidate | `{ imageUrl, thumbUrl, source, sourceUrl, licence, author }` from it | `{ kind: "url", url: imageUrl }` | its own | `"agent"` |
| Their file | `{ uploadId }`, with `sourceUrl` and a `note` naming the site when they say where it's from | `{ kind: "upload", uploadId }` | `"upload"` | `"person"` |
| Their public image link | `{ url, sourceUrl }` | `{ kind: "url", url }` | `"link"` | `"person"` |
| An image from their library | `{ url: imageUrl, sourceUrl: webUrl, note: "their library" }` | `{ kind: "url", url: imageUrl }` | `"library"` | `"person"` |
| A connector find they liked (web search included) | `{ url, sourceUrl, note }` or `{ uploadId, sourceUrl, note }`, `note` naming the connector | `{ kind: "url", url }` or `{ kind: "upload", uploadId }` | `"other"` | `"agent"` |

- **A link** must be a public https PNG, JPEG or WebP. Anything else is uploaded, except Pinterest's (`*.pinimg.com`) and Unsplash's (`images.unsplash.com`, `plus.unsplash.com`): those are refused on every path and redirect, through an image proxy that names one too, and are never downloaded and uploaded instead. A board save with one in it saves nothing: take it out and send the rest.
- **A provider `source`** (`pexels`, `openverse`, `commons`, `arena`) is kept only when the picture's host, or an upload's `sourceUrl` page, is that provider's. Otherwise it falls back to `"link"` for a url or `"upload"` for an upload, with no licence credit.
- **Uploads:** with a shell and the file on this machine, one `create_upload_link` per file with `direct: true`: run its `upload.curl` and use its `upload.uploadId`. Otherwise one link for up to 10 files: the person drops them all on its `pageUrl`, and `get_upload_link` lists their `uploadId`s.
- **A link that expires or carries a signature** (Mobbin's `image_url` lasts 30 days; never Pinterest's or Unsplash's, above): with a shell, download it and upload the file; with none, the person saves it and drops it on the upload page. One behind their login: the person saves and uploads it; never fetch it with their session or cookies.

## Without the finder

For a host that doesn't have `find_references` or the board tools yet. The same loop by hand:

- Steps 1 and 2 as written. The open sources come in only through their web search or links, each with its page as `sourceUrl` and the licence the page states. From an Are.na channel, ask for the images they want as links or uploads.
- Keep the board in the chat: a numbered list of each picture's link, page, source, licence, author and the person's reaction. A Pexels, Openverse or Commons page found by web search is still a web search find (`source: "other"`, `suppliedBy: "agent"`), with its licence and author from the page. Group the directions the same way.
- Hand off the same way: one direction per draft, five pictures at most, none shared, with each input's source fields. Put the sources and licences in your report.

## Known failures

- **Reshuffling one set.** Drafts made from subsets of the same few pictures converge, and the trial shows the same image in each column. Each draft gets its own pictures. With fewer than four likes, offer another round before planning drafts (three at most, step 6).
- **A connector's images with no source.** Every connector find keeps its page as `sourceUrl` and the connector in `note`, and the person hears where each came from. When Mobbin's answer carries an `ai_usage_notice`, show its text word for word and follow it: if it limits AI use of the screens, keep them off the board and out of Styles, and say why.
- **Search terms that name a subject.** "Coffee shop" finds coffee shops, and the subject leaks into every image. Name the look; the subject goes in the trial's prompts.
- **Licences ignored.** Keep `licence`, `author` and `sourceUrl` on every reference. Pexels: a link to Pexels (its `sourceUrl`) stays with the photo, and its photographer is credited where shown. CC BY and CC BY-SA: the author, the licence and a link; CC BY-SA also asks that changed versions be shared under the same licence, so say so when one is picked. A find with no stated licence (Are.na, a connector, a link) is used under its source's terms: say so, and leave it out if the person isn't sure they may use it.
- **More than five references in a draft.** It's refused. Keep the five that show the direction best, or split it into two directions.
- **Asking for a connector's password or key.** Never, for any service, and never put one into a call. If they paste one, don't use it: tell them keys don't belong in the chat and to revoke that one. They connect their own apps; you use what is connected.
- **Browsing a site we don't search.** Never open, search or scrape the five sites in step 1, or Mobbin, yourself (a web fetch, a browser, your own web search; downloading an image their Cosmos, Savee, Dribbble, Behance or Mobbin connector returned is fine), and never pass a page or image from them that you found as a `url`, `query` or `arenaChannel`. What the person brings from Cosmos, Savee, Dribbble, Behance or Mobbin, through their own connector, an upload or an image link they paste, is theirs. A Pinterest or Unsplash image is refused (`REFERENCE_URL_BLOCKED`, "Pinterest's terms don't let us keep its images, so this one can't be a reference"): tell them so, and never download and upload it to get round that. The refusal's "a picture the person made or owns" is never a saved copy of the pin or photo. Name what they liked in it, and find that in the open sources or their own photos.
- **Reactions the person didn't give.** Record only the likes and dislikes they named. If they ask you to choose, say which and why, and record them once they agree.
- **Mixing up numbers.** The finder numbers its candidates and the board its items. Ask and record by the board's.
- **Tiles missing from a sheet.** The finder's sheet holds its first 20 candidates (`listedOnly` are the rest). Those in `thumbnailsWaiting` weren't drawn because the account was reading many links: the same call after its `retryAfterSeconds` draws them, or judge them by title and `sourceUrl`. On a board's sheet, `contactSheet.waiting` items come with `get_reference_board` and those `items` after `waitSeconds`.
- **A full board.** A board holds 60 items. Before a save would pass that, say so and ask which of the new ones to leave out, or start a new board for the rest (`BOARD_FULL` saves nothing).
- **A timed-out save.** Send it again with the same `requestId`. On a first save a new one makes a second board.
- **A stale upload.** Uploads work for 24 hours, and only for the person who uploaded them: the board leaves others out of a direction's `inputs` (`unavailable`). For a trial started later: their own file, ask them to upload it again and add it to the board; a connector's find, get it again through the connector (its `sourceUrl`) and upload it; or leave it out.

## Limits

- `find_references` searches Pexels, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons, and reads an Are.na channel the person pastes (its first 50 items, leaving out what's re-hosted from Pinterest or Unsplash). Pexels is skipped, with a note, when the service has no Pexels key. 1 to 24 candidates a call (default 12), pages 1 to 10, 30 calls a minute per person, and each person has a share of each source's searches; a contact sheet holds 20 tiles. An `attribution` is 500 characters at most.
- We never search or browse Pinterest, Cosmos, Savee, Dribbble or Behance ourselves (the finder, a web fetch, a browser or our own web search), and Mobbin only through the person's own connector; what's from Cosmos, Savee, Dribbble, Behance or Mobbin comes in only as the person's own. Pinterest's and Unsplash's images are refused as references on every path. Brave image search isn't in the finder.
- A board holds 60 items. An item's `note` is 500 characters at most, its `licence` and `author` 200 each. A direction holds 2 to 5 pictures. A Style or draft keeps 5 references at most; a trial has at most 6 drafts.
- It finds and groups references. Making and testing the Styles is the `styles` skill.
